How privacy
actually works here.

Plain language. Not a promise
that the internet can’t keep.

01

Your searches go somewhere.

This demo sends your query to the Sreon API. The engine asks external search providers for results. The website host and those providers may process connection details.

02

Your preferences stay here.

This page stores your theme in this browser. It has no analytics script or advertising tracker. The hosting service may keep its own request logs.

03

A website is still a website.

Following a result takes you to that site, with its own cookies and privacy practices. Sreon is not a VPN or an anonymity service.

A FEW THINGS WORTH KNOWING

Just the
straight answers.

Is this a whole browser inside a website?

No. This is a small window into Sreon’s search engine. The desktop app handles actual browsing using your system’s webview. Here, results take you to the original websites.

Do I need an account to search?

No Sreon account is required. The public search service has request limits to keep it available, and external search sources may sometimes be unavailable.

Does Sreon have its own search index?

Not currently. The Rust engine retrieves results from existing online sources. Sreon is open about that distinction instead of claiming to crawl the whole web.

What stays on this device?

This page remembers your light or dark theme locally. It doesn’t save a search-history list in your browser. Queries still go to the search service and its providers; hosting services may keep connection logs.
